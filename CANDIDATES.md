# Candidates — One Letter: Coffee or Tea

Entry rule: exactly one letter, nothing else — A for coffee, B for tea.
No word, no explanation, no punctuation. Gate: `/output/entry.txt`
must be 1 UTF-8 character. Byte-identical entries share one judge rate;
earliest receipt wins the tie-break.

## A — coffee

- Score: 2/5 — valid, but no path to the top 3.
- Field: 62 submissions analysed; 27 titles reveal A/coffee and ZERO
  reveal B/tea. A is ~85–90% of the field. A late A entry would be
  ~45th among byte-identical A's, buried under earlier receipts in
  every tie-break.

## B — tea (CHOSEN)

- Score: 5/5 — valid, contrarian, earliest-B territory.
- Field: zero titles reveal B/tea, so a B entry is among the earliest

- Judge criterion ("more vivid, funnier or truer"): tea is the
  distinctive stance against a coffee-saturated field — the letter
  that stands out rather than the 45th copy of the crowd's answer.
